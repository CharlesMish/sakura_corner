# Visual thesis

This playground is becoming a Nocturne rain scene: the same urban sakura window,
cooled toward the sleep app's cyan air, with sakura pink and the shop gold kept
as the warm pair. Rain is the default. Clear remains `?weather=clear`.

The tree stays the star. Weather makes it feel sheltered, not soaked.

## What the viewer should feel

A quiet street in rain, at the hour you might leave Nocturne playing. Cool blue
air. Pink canopy. A warm shop nearby, and a smaller hearth further left, so the
picture has more than one dry room. A few moths live near that far window. Wet
sky dies into dark cyan instead of a bright grey horizon, with a few heavy rain
clouds behind the roofs.

## What changes

Materials and light do the heavy lifting. Pavement goes cooler and darker.
Ground oval puddles are out; they read as stains. A spout or sloped runoff can
come later. Rain is switchable: dash, pixel, or dense, via `?rain=`.

Sleep-app nods (hearth, moths) are named so a later mixer can mute them. They
are not a HUD.

## What stays quiet

Fixed camera. Pixelated illustration. No quests, characters, traffic, or neon
storefront conversion. The shop is still a neighborhood edge, not an onsen
lantern pasted on the street.

The far left is a quiet shrine hint (dark wood torii, small hall, stone lantern),
not a second hero. A low fence and gravel plot hold that precinct so it does not
sit on leftover sidewalk. The near left residence and its hearth stay; that house
now has a dark door, a balcony planter, and an alley with a gate and crates. Behind
the tree the street splits into plots, with a taller stair tower as the far crop.
Behind that, pavement gives way to earth, a hedge line, and a narrow canal so the
ground does not run out into empty slab.
